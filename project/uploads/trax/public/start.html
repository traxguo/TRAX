Questions

Frequently asked

Where do I download the app?

Nowhere — TRAX runs from your browser. Open the link, tap "Add to Home Screen", and it lives on your phone like a regular app. Updates arrive automatically.

Is my members' data safe?

Data is stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure. Each studio sees only its own data; you can export everything as a single file or delete your account entirely, anytime.

What happens when the trial ends?

The app lets you know. If you want to continue, you subscribe with your card in seconds; if not, you're charged nothing and your data waits for you for a reasonable period.

Does it work offline?

Yes — your latest data is stored on your device, so you can keep working without a connection. Everything syncs automatically when you're back online.

Can I use it on a computer?

Yes, the same account works in any browser. But TRAX shines brightest in your pocket — on the floor, at the door, between classes.
